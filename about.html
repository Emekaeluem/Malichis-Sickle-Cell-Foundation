Beyond The Diagnosis

Behind Every Diagnosis Is a Person.

There is a child trying to live a normal childhood. A parent watching over their child through another crisis. A young person carrying dreams despite the pain.

Sickle cell affects more than the body. It can affect education, relationships, finances, mental wellbeing and everyday life.

And that is why we exist.

Why Malichis Exists

Our Story

[Founder's name] saw someone they loved fight sickle cell disease without the medication, the emergency care, or even the basic information that could have made the fight survivable.

That's the problem we saw — and it's why Malichis Sickle Cell Foundation started in [year]: not as an organization first, but as a decision that no other family in Lagos should face that same struggle alone.

Since then, we've grown from a small group showing up at hospitals with medication in hand, to a foundation running awareness campaigns, education programs, and direct support for warriors across our community.

What began with a desire to help has grown into a mission to educate, support, advocate and create hope.

Education

Knowledge Can Save Lives.

Understanding sickle cell can change how people respond to it.

Through education and awareness, we help individuals, families, schools and communities understand sickle cell, challenge misconceptions and make informed decisions.

What We Do

Turning Compassion Into Action.

Educate

We provide accurate information and raise awareness about sickle cell.

Support

We stand with individuals and families navigating the realities of sickle cell.

Advocate

We speak up for better understanding, inclusion and support for people living with sickle cell.

Empower

We help people affected by sickle cell access knowledge, resources and a supportive community.

A Message Of Hope

They Are More Than Their Condition.

A sickle cell diagnosis does not define someone's dreams, abilities or future.

With understanding, support and access to the right resources, people living with sickle cell can pursue their dreams and live meaningful lives.

We believe in that future.
